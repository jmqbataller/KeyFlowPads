# KeyFlow Pads brand

The icon uses twelve 10 × 10 pads on a 64 × 64 grid. The four columns begin at x = 9, 21, 33, and 45. The three rows begin at y = 14, 27, and 40. The pads have 2.5-unit corner radii. The background has a 16-unit corner radius.

Two joined cubic Bézier segments run from left to right across the center row. A 6.5-unit charcoal stroke separates the curve from the pads, and a 2.7-unit white stroke keeps it visible at small sizes. The horizontal logo sets the Inter Bold wordmark to the right of the icon.

Use the primary wordmark on dark surfaces and the light-surface variant on light backgrounds. The monochrome SVG keeps the same geometry for limited-color use. The ICO includes 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 px sizes.
